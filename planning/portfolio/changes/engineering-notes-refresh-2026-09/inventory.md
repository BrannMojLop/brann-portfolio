# Existing Engineering Notes inventory

The repository contains 14 MDX files representing 7 English/Spanish pairs. All
pairs are dated `2026-03-09`. The classifications below are strategy decisions,
not permission to edit or publish them.

## Classification criteria

- `KEEP AS-IS`: relevant, supported, differentiated, and safe without a content change.
- `KEEP + LIGHT REFRESH`: preserve the thesis; later improve framing, links, freshness, or evidence labels.
- `EVIDENCE AUDIT REQUIRED`: valuable enough to preserve, but one or more factual, operational, production, or project-specific claims must be revalidated first.
- `REWRITE LATER`: preserve the topic, but the current structure or argument should be rebuilt around stronger evidence and judgment.
- `ARCHIVE CANDIDATE`: low differentiation or weak evidence; consider removal only after replacement, redirect, and SEO review.
