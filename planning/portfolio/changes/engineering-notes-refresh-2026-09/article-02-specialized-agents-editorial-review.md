## Strengths

- The opening frames one-agent risk as blurred responsibility and evidence, not
  as a contest between humans and AI.
- The article gives specialization a concrete meaning: bounded ownership,
  explicit inputs/outputs, and an authority boundary.
- Role sections distinguish orchestrator, strategy, domain/case-study,
  implementation, review, and evidence/QA responsibilities.
- Handoffs are explained as durable control points and shown with a sanitized
  schema rather than private prompts.

  independent review, technical validation, and a deferred release decision.

  from approval.

  promises that the workflow eliminates risk.

  and consequence.
