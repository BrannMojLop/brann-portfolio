operational systems. Its differentiator is the connection between software
architecture, backend systems, domain rules, and business operations.

platforms. Businext and TR0V1 remain selected evidence, the confirmed Head of
Systems role is explicit, and unverified scale, production, client-count, and
relative-date language is excluded.
