### Systems

**Intro:** Selected systems shaped by finance, credit, and enterprise operations.
Each case explains the business context, architectural decisions, technical
constraints, and bounded outcomes.

**Businext ERP:** A custom ERP platform built from scratch to support finance,
sales, inventory, human resources, dashboards, and accounting workflows. The
system brings multiple operational domains into a platform where business rules,
service boundaries, and backend evolution can be made explicit.

**TR0V1 Credit Platform:** A credit-management platform for credit lines,
installments, interest calculations, payments, and account statements. The
system centers on financial state, payment traceability, reconciliation, and
clear rules across credit workflows.

**Workforce:** An independent workforce-management product in development,
focused on organization context, authorization boundaries, and auditable people
workflows. Workforce is not production software.

### Sistemas

**Intro:** Sistemas seleccionados definidos por operaciones financieras,
crediticias y empresariales. Cada caso explica el contexto de negocio, las
decisiones de arquitectura, las restricciones técnicas y los resultados
acotados.

**Businext ERP:** Plataforma ERP personalizada construida desde cero para
soportar finanzas, ventas, inventario, recursos humanos, dashboards y flujos

pueden hacer explícitos.

**TR0V1 Credit Platform:** Plataforma de gestión de crédito para líneas de
crédito, parcialidades, cálculos de intereses, pagos y estados de cuenta. El

reglas claras en los flujos crediticios.

**Workforce:** Producto independiente de gestión laboral en desarrollo,
enfocado en contexto de organización, límites de autorización y flujos
auditables de personas. Workforce no es software en producción.
